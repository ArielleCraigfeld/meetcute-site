This policy explains what Meet Cute collects, why, who we share it with, and how you control it. Meet Cute is operated by Fruitful LLC (“we,” “us”). You must be 18 or older to use Meet Cute.

1. Information we collect

You give us

  • Account: email address and password, date of birth (used to confirm you are 18+), name.
  • Profile: photos, bio, pronouns, who you are interested in, relationship intent, and any other profile fields you complete.
  • Activity and quiz answers: your responses in onboarding and Love Lab activities — love language, schedule, lifestyle, date preferences, values and similar. These are what your compatibility scores are computed from.
  • Verification selfies: a live selfie captured during Selfie Verification. See §4.
  • Messages and reports: messages you send, and the contents of any report you submit.

Collected automatically

  • Approximate location: city-level only. We do not store precise GPS coordinates.
  • Device and usage: device model, OS version, app version, crash diagnostics, and in-app events used to operate and improve the app.
  • Purchases: transaction identifiers and subscription status from Apple. We never receive or store your payment card details.

From services you connect

  • Spotify (optional): if you connect Spotify, we receive your listening data to compute a music-taste component of your compatibility. You can disconnect at any time, and we delete the associated data.

2. How we use it

  • To create and operate your account.
  • To compute and explain compatibility between you and other members.
  • To show your profile to members matching your stated preferences, and theirs to you.
  • To operate the hearts currency, subscriptions, and in-app purchases.
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms, and keep members safe.
  • To confirm you are 18 or older.
  • To diagnose crashes and errors.

3. What other members can see

Your photos, name, age, pronouns, bio, and verification badge are visible to members you appear to in Discovery. Compatibility scores and the reasons behind them are shown to members viewing your profile. Your verification selfie is never shown to other members. Your email address, date of birth, and precise account details are never shown.

4. Selfie Verification

Selfie Verification confirms that a live selfie matches the photos on a profile. The badge describes that check and nothing more.

It is not a background check, identity check, or safety guarantee. A verified badge does not mean a member has been screened for criminal history or is safe to meet. Always take the same precautions you would with anyone you meet online.

Verification selfies are stored separately from your profile photos, are readable only by you and our systems, and are not published to your profile or shown to other members.

5. Who we share with

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, file storage, crash reporting, and push notifications. Data is processed on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Apple — purchase verification and push delivery.
  • Spotify — only if you connect it, and only to retrieve your listening data.
  • Advertising — [CONFIRM at submission: if third-party ads are disabled at launch, say so plainly here and amend before enabling them.]
  • Law enforcement and legal requests — where we are legally required to, or where necessary to prevent harm, including mandatory reporting of child sexual abuse material.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from Settings → Delete Account inside the app.

  • Your profile becomes invisible to other members immediately.
  • Your data is permanently deleted after a 7-day grace period, during which you can sign back in and restore the account.
  • After that window, your profile, photos, messages, and activity data are permanently removed.

We retain certain records longer where the law requires it or where necessary for safety investigations — including records associated with reports of abuse or child safety.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete Account, as described above.
  • Notifications: manage push notifications in Settings or in iOS.
  • Spotify: disconnect at any time in the app.
  • Tracking: iOS will ask before any tracking occurs; you can change this in iOS Settings.

8. Children

Meet Cute is for adults aged 18 and over. We verify date of birth at sign-up and do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we remove it. If you believe a minor is using Meet Cute, contact us immediately at our support page.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport, access controls on stored data, and server-side enforcement of who may read or write what. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
