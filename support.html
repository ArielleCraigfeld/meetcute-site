Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. Your profile disappears from Discovery immediately. You have 7 days to change your mind — sign back in during that window and you can keep your account. After 7 days the deletion is permanent.

What does the verified badge mean?

Selfie Verified means we confirmed a live selfie matches the photos on that profile. It is not a background check or a safety guarantee — see our Privacy Policy.

What are hearts?

Hearts are the in-app currency used for likes, super likes, and icebreakers. You earn them by logging in, completing daily goals, and playing Love Lab activities, and you can buy them in the app.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone to change or cancel. Refund requests go to Apple.

Why isn't my profile showing in Discovery?

Profiles appear once they have at least two photos and at least one completed activity. The app shows a banner telling you which one is missing.

Before launch: re-check every answer above against what the app actually does at submission, especially the discoverability requirement and the hearts earn paths — both have changed during development.
