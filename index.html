Match on who you are, not just how you look.

Meet Cute uses short games and quizzes to learn what actually matters to you — your love language, your schedule, how you like to spend a first date — and shows you why you and someone else fit.

How it works

  • Play a quick game. About ninety seconds, and it builds the start of your profile.
  • See real compatibility. Every score comes with the reasons behind it — never a number we made up.
  • Keep going in Love Lab. More activities mean better matches, and you can see exactly what changed.

Safety

Selfie Verification confirms a live selfie matches a profile's photos. Reporting and blocking are available from every profile and conversation, and reports are reviewed. See Support to get in touch.
